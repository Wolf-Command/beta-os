Names and scope only. **Do not implement in this phase.** Order is after the register grew past the 22-surface story. HR stays out. Finance SoD stays closed. Nothing posts. Nothing trades. `staff_ready` stays false until a pack is human-proved.

Vertical means one chain works from the first human action to the recorded result, on real rows, with the empty tables filled by that action. A new hub page is not a pack.

## 1. Owner cockpit, not another hub
One home. The 10 live read cards stay, with their UNKNOWN lines kept. Add the V1 questions the pulse dropped: Command Brief, R2 gap, system faults that open the job, team asks, Peaches on the same board. Each card opens the real desk from pack 2 onward, not a `-1` prove file. Wave 8 may keep trading and fitness out of staff IA; they stay on the Owner personal rail as desks, not mirrors.

## 2. Uma Blu buy → receive → petty → close
The OPEN outlet. Read Alchemist, then **write** receipt, petty, and a daily close into Beta tables that are currently 0. Recipe library is already copied; this pack does not pretend costing is done. Evidence attaches to the close. Approvals: one segregating step, because `core_approval_requests` is 0.

## 3. Atrium sales desk
The 14 views, on the 88 units / 570 leads / 399 payments, as tabs under AMG. Occ card remains vacant-vs-rooms until in-house is actually known. Kuta tonight board sits on the same hub. No housekeeping yet.

## 5. Atrium finance screens, still SoD

## 6. GGV nota → books

## 7. Guest stay middle
Only after pack 3. Reservations are not “1 row means no guests”. Find the authoritative PMS history or mark it UNAVAILABLE. Then pre-arrival and post-stay. Housekeeping is its own later pack.

## 8. Recipe variance

## 9. Pre-open outlets, config only

## 10. Approvals and evidence
Authority limits are 0. This pack is the gate the other packs call. It is not a new scoreboard.

## Explicitly not in this queue

## Done means
A named human can run the chain on Beta, the row is in the Beta store, the read-back matches, and failure shows on the card. Until then the function stays PARTIAL, SHELL, or MISSING.
