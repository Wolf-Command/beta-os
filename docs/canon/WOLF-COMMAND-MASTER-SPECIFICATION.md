# 1. Purpose and North Star

Wolf Command is the operating system for independently saleable businesses. Wolf Intelligence is the institutional intelligence layer across that operating system. The system exists to make GGV, AMG and BZG profitable, measurable, systemised, transferable, self-improving and progressively independent of founder intervention.

- Target sale structure: GGV, AMG and BZG are independently saleable.

- Target margins: GGV 70%; AMG 20%; BZG 20%. Margin targets are owner/shareholder visibility by default.

- Indicative valuation lens: approximately 5x EBITDA, with normalised EBITDA and quality-of-earnings evidence maintained continuously.

- Founder independence milestones: 7 days -> 30 days -> 90 days -> 365 days without unnecessary operational Matt intervention.

- Frontline usability standard: the 12-year-old-stoner principle - sophisticated underneath, obvious to operate.

- Daily operating principle: fewer priorities, executed properly. Default to three needle-moving priorities per role/unit per day.

# 2. Ownership, Governance and IP

Authority is derived from Role + Assignment + Scope + Limit. No one shares credentials. Delegations are explicit, time-bounded, scoped, logged and reviewable. Segregation-of-duties rules apply to sensitive workflows. Break-glass access is exceptional, audited and reviewed.

# 3. Wolf Constitutional Principles

- Wolf owns institutional memory; AI providers consume it but do not own it.

- DATA -> TOOLS -> REASONING -> SYNTHESIS -> HUMAN.

- Users see one coherent Wolf Intelligence experience, not model/provider complexity.

- Permissions are deterministic and server-enforced. AI is never the security layer.

- Human authority remains mandatory for material financial, legal, employment, permissions, public content and strategic decisions unless policy explicitly delegates otherwise.

- Red-team -> Beta -> Verify -> Human approval -> Production -> Measure.

- No undocumented glue. If a critical process depends on a mystery script/person, it is not engineered.

- DESIGNED is not BUILT; BUILT is not VERIFIED; VERIFIED is not LIVE.

- Every important answer or recommendation should be traceable to evidence where practical.

- Systems learn through governed progression: observation -> repeated pattern -> lesson candidate -> human review -> approved rule.

- Wolf should proactively surface important risks/opportunities but suppress low-value noise.

- Every material business promise, decision, exception and override should leave a defensible evidence trail.

- Every role is assessed through ELIMINATE -> AUTOMATE -> AUGMENT -> SIMPLIFY -> RETAIN.

- Build strategically important IP; buy commodity capability when it is better economics and reduces risk.

- Technology changes only when they make the organisation better, faster or more efficient.

# 4. Organisational Data Model

Core hierarchy: Group -> Legal Entity -> Brand / Business Unit -> Location -> Department -> Role -> Position -> Assignment -> Person. Person identity is permanent; jobs and role assignments can change without losing history.

# 5. Business Structure

## 5.1 AMG - Atrium Management Group

- Hotel operations use Yanolja as the authoritative PMS/operational source where its APIs expose the required data.

- AMG guest journey target: enquiry -> booking -> pre-arrival -> arrival -> stay -> requests/incidents -> checkout -> memory/photo -> relationship/return.

- Check-in/out should be swift, with pre-arrival completion possible before landing and staff finishing the human hospitality elements on arrival.

- Packages are first-class commercial products: stay+dive, stay+surf, stay+fish, honeymoon, wedding, birthday, engagement, fitness, retreat and future combinations.

- Daily hotel reporting becomes machine-generated operating state, not manual report writing.

- Goal: service so memorable it becomes a campfire legend told for eternity. Michelin Key ambition supports relentless improvement.

## 5.2 BZG - Blu Zone Group

- Each venue is treated as a mini-business with its own budget, P&L/profit centre, KPI set and dashboard; BZG Master rolls them up.

- ESB is the authoritative transactional source for POS/F&B operational facts and will represent each outlet separately.

- Central kitchen/stores and shared costs are allocated on evidence-based usage where possible.

- Service philosophy: Ritz-Carlton framework - ladies and gentlemen serving ladies and gentlemen. Reviews are earned, never requested.

- Operational philosophy: McDonald's repeatability without robotic hospitality.

## 5.3 GGV - Goss Group Ventures

- GGV is an agency/service business and internal shared marketing engine for AMG/BZG as well as external clients.

- Content Machine performs the heavy lifting: strategy, research, planning, asset requirements, workflow, learning and evidence; humans focus on judgment, creativity, relationships and execution.

- Client profitability is measured by client/contract/project/deliverable with staff cost and external cost allocation.

- Speed-to-lead is critical, especially for property investment leads. 24/7 triage can answer safely from approved facts and escalate hot leads.

- AMG and BZG behave as internal clients of GGV under formal service agreements, budgets, deliverables and arm's-length intercompany billing.

# 6. Identity, Access and User Experience

- Operational staff generally authenticate with their own personal email/identity; company email is not required merely to access Wolf.

- Generic role/business email addresses remain where customers/vendors need them. Shareholders may retain named company addresses.

- Email is a communication channel, not the organisation chart.

- Every dashboard is generated from role + scope + assignments rather than separate hard-coded apps per person.

- Frontline is mobile-first: Today / briefing / three priorities / tasks / scan / Ask Wolf.

- Complexity scales with responsibility. Finance/admin/GM views are more sophisticated; frontline views remain KISS.

- English is canonical. Indonesian operational translations are maintained; users may select interface language.

- New-person onboarding into Wolf is mandatory and role-driven: identity -> access -> values -> dashboard -> SOPs -> training -> competency -> first-shift checklist -> manager follow-up.

# 7. Wolf Intelligence

Wolf Intelligence is one institutional intelligence layer across GGV, AMG, BZG and Matt's executive command. It may privately use multiple models, tools, skills, retrieval systems and validators, while ordinary users only see Wolf Intelligence.

- Brand Memory / stable truth is separated from Learning Memory / changing observations and lessons.

- Fact, rule, observation, hypothesis, decision, lesson, inspiration and trend signal remain distinct evidence classes.

- Approved truth and deterministic rules outrank model-generated observations.

- Version history is preserved; superseded information is not silently overwritten.

- Ask Wolf exists throughout authorised dashboards and automatically uses role, context and permissions.

- Wolf may act directly only within delegated authority. Consequential actions are proposed and approved unless policy explicitly authorises automation.

- Autonomy expands only as capability reliability, audit, safety and business evidence mature.

- Wolf red-teams its own reasoning and major proposals; deterministic validation overrides AI where hard rules exist.

- Wolf can propose software/system improvements when recurring workarounds expose missing functionality, but production changes require Beta testing and approval.

# 8. SOP Factory, Training and Continuous Improvement

SOPs are executable operating standards, not static documents.

- Every SOP has owner, purpose, version, effective/review dates, preconditions, tools/materials, roles, workflow, visual good/bad examples, exceptions, escalation, evidence, KPIs, related SOPs, training and revision history.

- Execution divergence, repeated exceptions, software change and better staff methods can trigger SOP review proposals.

- Affected employees retrain after material SOP changes and may need to pass competency checks.

- Staff can suggest better workflows. Suggestions are valued, evidence-backed, reviewed and can become organisational IP with recognition/reward.

- The system should search across the organisation for similar occurrences after a lesson is learned.

# 9. HR and People Operating System

- Wolf HR becomes canonical employee/position/assignment truth per legal entity.

- Headcount is budgeted; manpower requests precede recruitment. Content Machine can prepare job ads for approval/posting.

- Recruitment, onboarding, probation, training, performance, leave, attendance, discipline, succession and offboarding are workflow-driven.

- Fingerprint attendance is current. Future phone attendance can be explored with anti-abuse controls.

- Performance scorecards use transparent declared metrics; no secret opaque AI employee score.

- Gamification can reward individual, team, department and company performance, with profit/health guardrails and anti-gaming controls.

- Core values: Lead by Example; Go the Extra Mile; Stay Humble and Curious; Build the Family; Bring the Energy and Belief.

- Credo: Excellence through the power of one more action.

- Recognition includes employee of month/year candidates, dinners, rewards and peer/guest recognition where evidence supports it.

- Succession, bus-factor risk, internal career paths and A-player development are first-class.

- Anonymous pulse surveys, suggestions and whistleblowing channels are supported with appropriate confidentiality.

# 10. Finance and Accounting

- Wolf Accounting is the official accounting system for each legal entity, separately maintained, with a standard group mapping for management consolidation.

- Outputs include P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, general ledger, trial balance, management accounts and tax-supporting records.

- Weekly bank statement uploads are the current default for Mandiri, BNI and BRI until practical banking integrations are proven.

- No payment is PAID until settlement evidence exists. Payment initiation may remain outside Wolf, while Wolf prepares verified payment runs and tracks confirmation.

- 13-week cash flow forecast runs per entity and updates as new evidence arrives.

- Annual budget + rolling forecast + 12-week execution + department/venue/project budgets.

- Three-way match by default: PO -> Goods Received -> Supplier Invoice. Material mismatches block/escalate payment.

- Daily operational close, monthly accounting close, quarterly review and annual close. Month-end management accounts targeted by the 5th.

- Closed periods lock; reopening requires authority and audit.

- Manual journals are exceptional, explained and risk-appropriate approved.

- Profit leakage is continuously identified: waste, theft/variance, overtime, poor purchasing, discounts, refunds, empty/OOO rooms, unprofitable menu items, underpriced contracts, overservicing, failed marketing, subscription waste and preventable maintenance.

# 11. Procurement and Suppliers

- Workflow: request -> approval -> quotes/RFQ -> PO -> ordered -> receiving -> invoice match -> payment approval -> paid -> ledger.

- Above configurable thresholds, multiple quotes are required. Emergency purchase override is allowed with reason, evidence and retrospective review.

- Supplier intelligence covers price history, quality, reliability, delivery time, discrepancies, payment terms, disputes and alternatives.

- Receiving is mobile: PO, count/weight, photos where appropriate, temperature/quality checks, partial deliveries, substitutions, discrepancy reason, acceptance/rejection.

- Segregation of duties prevents one person controlling supplier setup, PO, receipt, invoice approval and payment approval end-to-end.

- Wolf scans the market for better commercial opportunities and proposes changes with business case and red-team analysis.

# 12. BZG F&B Control System

- Every menu item has recipe/BOM, yield/waste assumptions, theoretical cost, current margin and menu-engineering analysis.

- ESB remains detailed F&B stock/transaction truth; Wolf controls intelligence, finance, procurement, risk and cross-system workflows.

- Stock counts are risk-based. High-value/high-risk items count more frequently; theoretical vs actual variances create investigation tasks.

- Waste, spoilage, staff meals, comps, returns, remakes, discounts, refunds, voids, overrides and cash variance are explicitly recorded and analysed.

- Staff never share ESB logins. Manager approval fobs are preferred over stealable PINs where ESB supports them.

- Cash chain is tracked from opening float -> shift count -> safe -> deposit -> bank statement with timestamps and accountable people.

- Suspicious events can produce CCTV review windows; Wolf identifies anomalies/evidence, humans investigate.

- Labour is measured by outlet/shift/revenue, with demand-based staffing recommendations and profitable-selling incentives balanced with service/reviews.

# 13. AMG Hotel Operating System

- Yanolja owns reservation/PMS facts; Wolf uses APIs to orchestrate staffing, tasks, intelligence, reporting and cross-company workflows.

- Daily report fields include occupancy, rooms sold, ARR, rooms saleable, OOO/OINV, room/F&B/other revenue, MTD/YTD, arrivals, departures, events, seven-day forecast and reliable competitor/compset evidence where available.

- Front office receives prioritised arrivals/departures/VIPs/room readiness/balances/requests/transfers/complaints/handover.

- Housekeeping receives optimised mobile room queues, inspections, room-damage/theft/smoking checks before checkout and workload forecasting.

- Guest requests become trackable service items with SLA/escalation where appropriate.

- Reviews are not solicited. Remarkable service should earn them organically; review evidence feeds recognition, training and process improvement.

- Guest photography is part of the experience: consent/check-in documentation, safe capture, one memorable image, watermarked optional gallery, purchase flow; photography economics attributed to GGV.

# 14. Customer, CRM and Package Intelligence

- Wolf uses a canonical Customer ID / identity graph, with reversible confidence-based matching across Yanolja, ESB, CRM and channels. Human review is required when identity is uncertain.

- Client/customer data boundaries override identity matching. One GGV client cannot gain access to another client's lead/customer data.

- Packages are structured products with components, capacity, supplier/internal cost, selling price, margin, tax/service-charge treatment, cancellation terms, owner and SOPs/tasks.

- Lead triage is 24/7 where authorised. Money likes speed: approved facts can be answered immediately; uncertainty escalates instead of hallucinating.

- CRM activity is captured automatically where possible from email, connected messaging, voice notes and meetings; humans confirm uncertain interpretations.

- High-value/low-cost extras are preferred to discounting. Below-margin deals escalate.

- Customer portal is lightweight web/PWA, cross-platform, with pre-check-in, itinerary, transfers, experiences, requests, bills, photos, concierge and event planning as relevant.

# 15. Maintenance, Assets, Security and Compliance

- One Wolf Maintenance system can serve AMG/BZG while preserving entity ownership, arm's-length economics and work records.

- Every maintainable asset has permanent Asset ID/barcode and record: owner, site, location, model/serial, purchase, supplier, warranty, manuals, photos, maintenance history.

- Tropical/dusty/construction-heavy conditions influence maintenance intervals; manufacturer schedules are inputs, not unquestioned truth.

- Repair-vs-replace considers repair cost + downtime + revenue/guest impact + reliability history.

- Areas can also have scan points for inspections/checklists where useful.

- Security maintains incidents, patrols/checkpoints, keys, visitor/contractor access, CCTV registry/health, evidence timelines and access audit.

- Compliance register covers corporate, licences, food safety, fire/safety, insurance, employment/BPJS, tax obligations, data/privacy, vendor evidence and other applicable obligations.

- Critical emergency SOPs are drilled; drill weaknesses become process/training improvements.

- Every major emergency gets a Preventability Review and root-cause corrective action.

# 16. Documents, Records and Legal Defensibility

- All business documents/records must be easily callable through Wolf according to sensitivity and approval.

- Documents are context-aware records, not mystery files: entity, related object, authority/version, dates, sensitivity, permissions, provenance and retention.

- Important business decisions preserve a court-defensible chain: original, versions, author, approvals, communications, evidence, execution and result.

- Ordinary users cannot permanently destroy audit evidence. Deletion generally archives/tombstones; legal hold overrides normal deletion.

- Wolf can reconstruct a dispute chronology automatically from related records.

- Contract intelligence extracts structured obligations, dates, prices, notice windows, service levels and owners, then creates tasks/alerts.

# 17. Strategy, 12-Week Year and Executive Command

- Strategy hierarchy: long-term vision -> 3-year -> annual -> 12-week company -> department -> individual commitments -> daily three priorities.

- Activity completion is distinguished from outcome achievement. A fully completed bad plan is still a bad plan.

- Every KPI has versioned definition, formula, source, owner, frequency, target, thresholds, history, benchmark and evidence.

- Wolf searches for leading indicators and uses evidence to test whether they are actually predictive.

- Exceptions use severity: INFO -> WATCH -> ACTION -> URGENT -> CRITICAL. Behaviour varies by severity.

- Executive dashboard uses Pulse + Exceptions. Matt sees bird's-eye direction until satisfied, then progressively receives fewer operational interruptions.

- For material problems, Wolf should normally present 2-3 evidence-backed courses of action plus the option to propose another approach.

- Founder interruptions/week, founder operational hours and founder-dependency categories are sale-readiness KPIs.

# 18. Personal Command Boundary

- Matt personal domain is strictly segregated from company records and permissions.

- Private domains include health, sleep/recovery, fitness, family, relationships, calendar, personal goals, dream boards, travel, investments/trading analysis and personal documents.

- Company users see availability, not private reasons.

- Founder independence is gamified as a Mission-to-Mars-style progression; protected downtime and recovery are design objectives, not rewards after burnout.

- Personal health intelligence supports tracking and preparation, not medical diagnosis.

# 20. Technology and Capability Governance

- Technology Register fields: why it exists, owner, vendor, cost, renewal, users, data, integration, criticality, backup, exit/export, replacement candidate, last/next review.

- Capability lifecycle: IDEA -> SPECIFIED -> APPROVED -> BUILDING -> BETA -> RED TEAM -> PILOT -> VERIFIED -> LIVE -> REVIEW -> RETIRED; DEGRADED is an operational state.

- Capability Registry prevents Wolf from claiming abilities that are only designed, not live.

- Buy-vs-build decisions compare cost, speed, lock-in, API, reliability, portability, maintenance and strategic value.

- Cumulative cost of the entire Wolf build and future upgrades is tracked.

- Technical Debt Register tracks fragile-but-working components, risk, business impact, remediation estimate and owner.

- Not Building / Not Yet register records shiny ideas, reasons and reconsideration triggers.

# 21. Build and Commissioning Philosophy

- No giant-bang rewrite. Build Wolf Core once and deliver controlled vertical slices with real business outcomes.

- Vertical Slice #1: Content Machine. Generic identity, permission, evidence, audit, memory, task and intelligence capabilities are extracted into Wolf Core rather than re-created later.

- Vertical Slice #2 candidate: BZG / Uma Blu.

- Vertical Slice #3 candidate: AMG / Yanolja operations.

- Finance + Procurement then span all entities as common Wolf domains.

- Each feature defines acceptance criteria before build, including bad paths, permissions, API failure, duplicate actions, staff mistakes and misuse cases.

- Beta includes frontline users and measures friction, confusion, workarounds, completion time and support questions.

- Legacy parallel systems have explicit sunset dates after verified migration; shadow systems are integrated, replaced or banned deliberately.

Default prioritisation logic: BUSINESS RISK + ECONOMIC IMPACT + FOUNDER DEPENDENCY + OPERATIONAL PAIN + STRATEGIC ENABLEMENT - BUILD COST/RISK. Opening-critical capabilities can jump the queue. Theft/fraud/control risks generally outrank nice dashboards. UX quality remains a hard adoption requirement.

# 24. Sale Readiness and Buyer Independence

- Each company maintains continuous sale-readiness evidence across finance, earnings quality, customer/supplier concentration, management depth, founder dependency, SOP/training coverage, compliance, contracts, data quality, technology portability, cyber, assets, IP, succession and growth.

- Normalised EBITDA schedule is maintained continuously with evidence. Reported EBITDA and normalised/sale EBITDA remain clearly distinct.

- Founder dependency categories: NONE / OVERSIGHT / APPROVAL / OPERATIONAL DEPENDENCY / PERSONAL RELATIONSHIP / KNOWLEDGE DEPENDENCY.

- Continuously updated virtual data room means sale due diligence becomes controlled access, not six months of document hunting.

- Buyer mode exposes only approved company-specific due diligence data and Ask Wolf context. Wolf crown jewels and cross-company intelligence remain hidden.

- Buyer Attack Audit red-teams the company before a sale process and fixes weaknesses rather than hiding them.

- Wolf licence can continue after sale; buyer owns/receives the business data and approved business knowledge, while generic Wolf IP/architecture remains Matt-owned.

# 25. Security, Recovery and Crown Jewels

- Secrets never live in SOPs/chats/ordinary documents. Use proper secrets management, MFA, hardware keys where practical and audited access.

- Company devices are assets. BYOD is allowed for controlled lower-risk workflows; sensitive functions can require managed devices/stronger authentication.

- Development, staging/Beta and production are separated. Production data is not casually copied into development; sanitised test data is preferred.

- Nothing is considered backed up until restore has been tested. Recovery uses tiered RTO/RPO targets and independent failure domains for crown-jewel data.

- Matt-only crown-jewel recovery should use proper encrypted multi-party/secret-sharing recovery. A book-code can be ceremonial/disguised input, not the cryptographic foundation.

- Only Matt has the full Wolf backup playbook and Technology/Provider Registry by default.
